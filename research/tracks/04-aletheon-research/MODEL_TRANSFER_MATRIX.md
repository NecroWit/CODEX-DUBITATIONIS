Decision recorded 2026-10-19. Research design, not executed.

Same-model fresh-context continuations (X → X, Y → Y) test continuity without changing model. Cross-model continuations (X → Y, Y → X) test portability in both directions.

Use two models of comparable capability, preferably from different families. Verify both can independently complete the selected tasks. Keep Core, state handoff format, tasks, acceptance criteria, and context budgets comparable. Do not provide the full transcript to the continuation model.

Pilot proposal: 10–15 tasks per cell (40–60 handoffs), preferably the same tasks across all cells. This is not a statistically powered confirmatory sample. Log successes, failures, usage and costs; later calculate sample size accounting for paired tasks and the chosen primary comparison.
